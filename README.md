### 3. AI 落地 · AI Solutions

**适合：** 想省时间的老板和小团队
**For:** owners and small teams who want their time back

把重复的回复、跟进和内容制作交给 AI，从第一个能马上见效的流程开始。
Hand repetitive replies, follow-ups, and content production to AI, starting with the workflow that pays off fastest.

- **AI 客服助手**：在网站上自动回答常见问题、协助预约，有需要时转给你本人
  **AI assistant:** answers common questions and helps book appointments, handing off to you when needed
- **咨询自动跟进**：表单一进来就自动回复、整理到表格、提醒你跟进
  **Automated lead follow-up:** instant replies, organized lead lists, and follow-up reminders
- **AI 内容工作流**：一套流程，让你和团队用更少时间产出更多内容
  **AI content workflow:** a repeatable process to create more content in less time
- **定制小工具**：报价器、计算器、互动测试，帮你获客也帮你省事
  **Custom tools:** quote builders, calculators, and quizzes that win leads and save time
- **AI 团队培训**：用听得懂的方式，教老板和员工把 AI 用在日常工作里
  **AI team training:** plain-language training so you and your team use AI every day
- **AI 落地评估**：先找出最耗时的环节，再决定从哪里开始
  **AI readiness assessment:** find your biggest time drains first, then decide where to start

**我的原则 · Principles**
先找最省时间的环节，不为了用 AI 而用 AI；重要决定和客户沟通始终有人把关；事先说清楚数据存在哪里、谁能访问；做完教会你自己用，不让你依赖我。

Start with the biggest time drain, never AI for its own sake. Keep a human in the loop for important decisions and client conversations. Be clear upfront about where your data lives and who can access it. Train you to run it yourself, so you're never dependent on me.
